How Rotimatic NEXT Works

From raw whole wheat flour to a golden puffed roti in under 90 seconds. 10 software-calibrated motors orchestrate each roti with micro-millimeter precision.

Add Flour, Water & Oil

0 sec setup

Load your preferred whole wheat, multigrain, or gluten-free flour into the separate top airtight canisters. Add water and oil once — enough for 20+ rotis per fill.

Sensors: Optical Flour Density Check
2

Machine Kneads & Presses

~45 seconds

Rotimatic's micro-dosing pump mixes the exact flour-to-water ratio. A spiral robotic blade kneads a fresh dough ball for every single roti before heated plates press it flat.

Force: 400 kg Uniform Pressure
3

Fresh Roti Out in Seconds

Under 90 sec

Dual contact heating plates bake both sides evenly. Moisture is vaporized inside the dough, creating that legendary aromatic balloon puff before sliding hot onto your plate.

Result: 100% Puffed, Piping Hot Roti
