HOW IT WORKS

How Rotimatic NEXT Works

From flour to a hot, puffed roti in under 90 seconds — mixing, kneading, pressing, and baking all happen automatically inside the machine.

Product demo: fresh rotis made automatically, unedited.
1

Add Flour, Water & Oil

0 sec setup

Load your preferred whole wheat, multigrain, or gluten-free flour into the separate top airtight canisters. Add water and oil once — enough for 20+ rotis per fill.

Door open: internal mixing chamber where flour, water, and oil come together.
2

Machine Kneads & Presses

~45 seconds

The machine doses flour and water automatically, kneads a fresh dough ball for each roti, and presses it flat between heated plates.

Top open: sealed canisters hold flour, water, and oil between refills.
3

Fresh Roti Out in Seconds

Under 90 sec

Heated plates bake both sides evenly, so the roti puffs up the way it would on a tawa, and slides out hot and ready to eat.

Result: 100% Puffed, Piping Hot Roti
